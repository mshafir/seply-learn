`/` needs a session (`GET /api/me`); signed out, it redirects to `/sign-in`. `/e/*` opens signed out too: a public or unlisted Expedition reads without a login (read-only, with "Sign in" in the header); a private one is "not found", with a Sign in button. The last signed-in user is kept in `localStorage` (`seply-user`) so a reload without a connection can still load that user's pending edits.

### The Expedition screen

- **Header** (`h-14`): the glyph back to the Library, the title (owners and editors rename it in place, through the sync client), **search inside the Expedition** ("Search text or #tag", `src/expedition/concept-search.tsx`: client-side over the loaded Concepts' titles, aliases, summaries and Tags, so it works offline; every word must match, `#tag` by Tag prefix; the canvas dims the rest through `ExpeditionView`'s `matches`, a badge counts matches, Escape clears; `matchConcepts` in `src/lib/search.ts`, unit tested), sync status ("Saving", "Offline · N edits waiting") and the **account menu** (name, theme System / Light / Dark, sign out).
- **Views bar**: `views-bar.tsx`, under the header (`--views-bar-height: 3rem`): a tab per View (View Type icon and name) with a Hover Card for the question, the View Type's description and the build status; the tabs that don't fit go into a "N more" DropdownMenu. The open View keeps its tab. The same at every width.
- **Canvas pane**: as wide as possible. `src/expedition/canvas-slot.tsx` mounts `@seply/views`' `ExpeditionView` on the sync client's collections with the selected View and Concept. The **View button** (icon, name, question, settings icon) floats over its top-left corner and opens the View panel; the View is drawn below it.
- **Side panel**: a collapsible panel in the shadcn Resizable group, opened by selecting a Concept (the Concept panel) or by the View button (the View panel). It slides open and shut (a `flex-grow` transition applied only around the app's own changes, never while dragging; off under reduced motion), is 520 px by default and 860 px while reading a full article, and each width is kept per browser. Below 1024 px it is a right-hand Sheet.
- **View panel** (spec §3.6, §4.2, WP-2.4): `src/expedition/view-panel.tsx`. The description (the View Type doc's opening paragraph), **shared settings** (owners and editors only), **your settings**, "Duplicate" (editors: a copy of the View next to it, as new ops) and "Read the View Type" (the `docs/view-types/<id>.md` file, bundled at build time by `src/expedition/view-type-docs.ts`). Both forms are generated from the View Type's Zod schemas in `@seply/domain` (`src/expedition/settings/`): `fields.ts` walks a schema into fields (unit tested), `settings-form.tsx` draws them with shadcn Field, Switch, Select, Checkbox, Input and Textarea. The per-View structure overrides (`placement`, `order`, `hide`, `fold`) are not in the form. A shared change is checked against the schema, then written to the View row; the sync client turns it into `view.set` ops by settings path.
- **Personal View settings**: `src/lib/personal-view-settings.ts`. Per reader and View, never shared, defaults from the View Type's personal schema. Read and written through the `PersonalViewSettingsStore` interface; the screen passes the reader's store (`ReaderPersonalViewSettings` in `src/lib/reader.ts`), so they are saved in `personal_view_settings` through the reader API and follow the reader across devices (an anonymous reader's stay in this browser). The localStorage store is the fallback. They reach the View as `personal` (Learning path's "Show all steps" is the same setting as its toolbar toggle; "Hide what I've read" hides covered Concepts).
- **Status chip** (`src/expedition/status-chip.tsx`): View-specific status floating at the bottom of the canvas, e.g. "Path to MLA · 7 of 11 read" (counting the reader's Reading status), with a clear button; the View reports it through `onStatus`.
- **Reading status** (spec §3.7, WP-2.5): under the Concept's title, a ToggleGroup: Not read yet / Read / I knew this. Read and known Concepts get a check in every View (`covered`, passed to `ExpeditionView` with the View's personal settings); the Learning path skips them in its steps, and "Hide what I've read" removes them. Signed out, after the first mark: "Sign in to keep your progress across devices."
- **Reader state** (`src/lib/reader.ts`, `src/components/reader-provider.tsx`): one `@seply/sync` `ReaderClient` per page for whoever is reading (the user, or the browser's anonymous reader), over the IndexedDB reader store. Signed in, marks are saved through `/api/reader` (queued in IndexedDB while offline, saved on reconnect); anonymous marks stay in the browser, and move into the account on sign-in (newest wins). The screen refreshes the reader's state on open and whenever the tab comes back into view (other devices' marks); other tabs hear marks at once.
- **Continue reading**: the screen saves my position (View, the Concept in the panel and its depth) a moment after I stop moving. Opened without a View in the URL, it lands there, with "Continuing where you left off · Back to the start" (the best View, panel closed). Pure parts in `src/expedition/continue.ts` (unit tested).
- **Concept panel** (spec §3.7, WP-1.7): `src/expedition/concept-panel.tsx`, with the pure parts in `reading.ts` (unit tested, `pnpm --filter web test`). A back stack of places: the overview depth (summary, Tags, aliases, Attributes, the overview, "Read the full article", Relationships both ways: "Links to" with each Relationship Type's label, "Linked from" with its inverse label) and the article depth (sections in order). Markdown renders with react-markdown + remark-gfm inside shadcn Typeset (`src/expedition/prose.tsx`; raw HTML is not rendered). In-text `#c/<id>` links and Relationship links push onto the stack; Back pops it; a canvas click starts a new stack. Every overview and article section has a provenance badge: "From the chat, turn N" (segment ids per the seeding contract: `tN` chat turn, `sN` document section) or "Background knowledge". `p2` is a PDF page, and a split segment (`t3a`) reads as its whole. A badge whose Source the Expedition has is a link: it opens the **Source viewer**.
- **Source viewer** (spec §3.7, WP-3.1): `src/expedition/source-viewer.tsx`, a Dialog over the screen (so closing it returns to the panel as it was), with the pure parts in `source-reading.ts` (unit tested). It loads `GET /api/sources/:expedition/:source` and shows the Source's title, a summary ("Pasted chat · 6 turns", "PDF · 3 pages"), the cited segment ("Cited: Turn 5") and "Download the file"; then the segments: chat turns with who said it ("You", or the assistant by its export: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) and "Turn N", conversation titles where an export's conversations change, document sections under their headings, PDF pages. The cited segment (every part of a split one) is highlighted (`--suggested`) and scrolled into view. Long Sources draw a window around it (20 before, 40 after), with "Show earlier" / "Show more". A link opened in a new tab (`?source=<id>&segment=<id>`) opens the viewer on load. Offline, or for a Source without stored text (imported from JSON), it says so.
- **Offline reading** (spec §2.9, WP-2.7): the app is an installable PWA (`vite-plugin-pwa`, `vite.config.ts`): the service worker precaches the app shell (HTML, JS, CSS, the self-hosted fonts' Latin subsets, the brand icons), falls back to `index.html` for client-side routes, and caches no API response. Expeditions for offline reading live in IndexedDB (`src/lib/offline.ts`, `@seply/sync`'s `OfflineCache`, database `seply-offline`): the last 10 opened, plus any pinned in the Library, per user. The screen saves the confirmed state when it opens an Expedition and a moment after each change. Opened when the server can't be reached, an Expedition with a saved copy reads from it **read-only** (no renaming, no shared settings, no Duplicate), with the **"Offline, as of … · read-only" chip** in the header (`src/expedition/offline-chip.tsx`, with Retry); the header still counts edits waiting from before. Reading status and position marks still queue (per ticket 16). The screen keeps retrying, at once when the browser comes back online, and the live client replaces the copy. Without a copy it says it can't reach the server. Signing out clears the user's saved Expeditions.
- **Building** (spec §3.5, WP-3.7): the Expedition opens while it builds. `src/expedition/use-builds.ts` listens to the screen's room (below) while signed in and online, and fetches `GET /api/expeditions/:id/jobs` on each `hello` and whenever a job starts, pauses or ends; a build's commits arrive as `ops` or `poke` like any other edit. `build-state.ts` (pure, unit tested) folds the events and jobs into each View's status: `queued` ("Waiting its turn"; "Paused at the spending cap"), `building` (the step, progress and streamed Concepts), `ready`, `failed` (the reason: the event's, else the logged `failReason`), or `stopped` ("Not built": queued or building with no job running, left by Cancel, Stop or a job that failed on another View). The newest event about a View wins over its logged status until the pull arrives; a job queued again (Retry, Continue) forgets its old View events.
  - **Rail**: a status Badge per View (Queued, Building with a pulsing dot, Failed, Not built; nothing when ready), then the step and an amber Progress, or the reason.
  - **Canvas**: a View that isn't ready shows its **skeleton** (`view-skeleton.tsx`, per View Type family: reserved graph slots, table rows with "?" cells, outline structure, a framed map with pins and open rings, an axis, a quadrant grid) with the streamed Concepts filling it; a failed or stopped View shows the **failed-View card** (`failed-view-card.tsx`: the reason, then Retry (the newest failed or cancelled job's next attempt; it skips ready Views), Try another View (a DropdownMenu of View Types: a queued View of that type takes the failed one's place and question, then Retry), Remove). Actions are for owners and editors.
  - **First ready opens by itself**: opened without a View in the URL, the screen shows the best View once it is ready, else the first ready one (`startViewOf`), so the first to finish replaces the skeleton. A toast announces each View as it finishes (with "Open" when it isn't the one on screen), each failure, and "Every View is built".
  - **Header activity indicator** (`build-activity.tsx`): while a job is queued, running or paused, "Building · 1 of 3 Views ready" (a Spinner; "Paused" at the cap) opens a Popover with the job's step, its Progress, each View's status, **Cancel** (keeps the finished Views; toast) and **Leave it building**: a Dialog ("Notify me" calls `enablePush()` and the browser asks; "Just leave"), then the Library, with a toast when notifications can't be had.
  - **Spending cap**: a paused job shows an Alert at the top right of the canvas, "Paused at the spending cap · Spent $0.50 of the $0.50 cap", with **Continue** (`POST /api/jobs/:id/continue`) and **Stop** (cancel); also in the activity Popover.
- **Suggestions** (spec §1.5, §3.8, WP-4.3): owners and editors online get a **Suggestions** button in the header with the pending count ("Suggestions · 6"), which opens the Suggestions tab (`src/expedition/suggestions-panel.tsx`, a `PanelContent` of type `suggestions`). `src/expedition/use-proposals.ts` fetches `GET /api/expeditions/:id/proposals` on open, on window focus, every 30 s while visible, after each review, when an article ask finishes, and on every room `poke` (the server pokes the room whenever Proposals change, so other tabs and collaborators see reviews live); `ingest(proposal)` merges one from elsewhere (WP-4.4's `data-proposal` parts). Items are grouped by ask (its rationale, "Your ask" / "Ana's agent (via MCP)", time) and described in plain words (`src/expedition/suggestions.ts`, unit tested: "New Concept · KV cache", "KV cache is needed to understand MLA", "Article for MLA · 3 sections", "Summary of MLA"). Each has **Accept** and **Dismiss**; each ask has **Accept all** and **Dismiss all**, and the header **Accept all** takes every ask. `planAccept` includes the dependencies (a Relationship's new Concept), marks stale ones (an overwrite) and leaves out what can't apply; when it adds, overwrites or leaves out anything, a dialog shows it before confirming. A stale item shows "Changed since suggested" with **Now** and **Suggested**, and "Accept and overwrite"; one whose Concept was deleted can only be dismissed. Accepting calls `POST …/proposals/review` (one Change on the server), pulls it, and toasts "Accepted 2 suggestions" with **Undo**; undoing an accept (from the toast or History) calls `…/proposals/reopen` with its Change id so its items are pending again. Dismissing toasts "Dismissed 1 suggestion" with Undo (reopen by item ids). **Preview:** while the tab is open, and while reading a Concept opened from it, the canvas and panels read `usePreview`: the live state with every pending item applied (`previewProposals`) in a read-only client (`openCachedClient`, the View-as-of pattern), and the Views draw the suggested Concepts and Relationships dashed in `--suggested`; nothing is editable meanwhile. A new MCP Proposal arriving after the first load toasts "Your agent (via MCP) suggested 2 Concepts" with the ask as its description and **Review** (the toast's close is "Later").
- **Live** (spec §2.4, §3.6, WP-4.1):
  - **The room** (`src/expedition/use-room.ts`): one `@seply/sync` `RoomClient` per open Expedition, for every reader online (signed in or not; none on the offline copy). `ops` go straight into the sync client (`receiveRelayed`); `hello` and `poke` pull only when the log has moved past the client's head. While the room isn't open (connecting, a server without a live room) the client pulls every 3 s while the page is visible, and at once when it becomes visible again; once it is open, nothing polls. The tab says `leave` on `pagehide`. A `kick` toasts "Disconnected" and reopens the screen, which checks access again.
  - **Presence avatars** (`presence-avatars.tsx`, the list from `use-presence.ts`): in the header before the account menu, one shadcn Avatar per other person in the room (never me, nor my other tabs), in their colour (one of the 12 Kind hues, from their user id: `presence.ts`), with a Tooltip "Ed Hopper, on Learning path"; an agent via MCP shows with a bot icon, last. Hidden when nobody else is here. Only collaborators see it (readers of a link hear no presence).
  - **Live cursors** (`live-cursors.tsx`, DIVERGENCES #4): the other collaborators' pointers on the View I have open, over the canvas pane, in their colour with a Badge name tag. A cursor is sent as fractions of the pane plus, over a Concept (`[data-concept]`, which every View renderer sets), fractions of that Concept's box, and drawn on the same Concept when my View shows it (else at the same place in the pane), repositioned every animation frame so it follows pans and zooms. Sending (`useCursorSender`) and the View and selection go through `RoomClient.setPresence`, throttled to 15 Hz; leaving the pane clears the cursor.
- **History** (spec §1.4, §3.9, WP-4.2): owners and editors get a **History** button in the header (`expedition-header.tsx`), which opens the History side panel (`src/expedition/history-panel.tsx`, a `PanelContent` of type `history`). `src/expedition/use-history.ts` fetches `GET /api/history` (`getHistory` in `src/lib/api.ts`) while the panel is open (or a point is being viewed), again a moment after the log moves, and keeps older pages loaded with "Show older Changes". Each Change shows its label, author ("You" for me) and time ("Ana · 2h ago", `src/expedition/history.ts`, unit tested) and has **Undo**, **View as of here** and **Restore to here** (the newest has only Undo). Undo and Restore call the sync client's `undo(changeId)` / `restoreTo(lastSeq)` with the labels "Undid “…”" / "Restored to “…”" and toast the result; Undo's toast reports kept edits ("1 edit kept: changed since by Ed", `keptMessage`). **View as of here** replays the log up to the Change's `lastSeq` (`client.stateAsOf`) into a read-only client (`openCachedClient`), which the canvas, panels and Views bar read instead of the live one; an "As of “…”" bar under the Views bar offers Restore to here and Back to latest. While viewing, nothing is editable. Not offered on a saved offline copy (no log).
- **Web push** (spec §2.5, WP-3.2): `src/lib/push.ts` has `enablePush()` (asks permission, subscribes this browser with the server's VAPID key from `/api/web-push/key`, registers it at `/api/web-push/subscriptions`; returns `enabled`, `denied`, `unsupported` or `unavailable` when the server has web push off) and `disablePush()`. "Leave it building" (WP-3.7) calls `enablePush()`. The service worker imports `sw/push-sw.js` (emitted as `/push-sw.js` by a small plugin in `vite.config.ts`), which shows a job's notification (`{ title, body, url, tag }`) and opens or focuses its Expedition on click.
- **Data**: `useSyncClient(expeditionId, userId)` (`src/lib/sync.ts`) opens `@seply/sync`'s client with the fetch transport and the IndexedDB pending store, retries opening with backoff (1 s doubling to 30 s) when the server can't be reached, and disposes it on unmount. Other tabs' and people's edits arrive through the live room (below). `useExpeditionData(collections)` gives the live rows (`useLiveQuery` per collection) for the rail, header and panel.

## Notes

- **Theme:** `index.html` has an inline script that sets `.dark` and `color-scheme` before first paint, from the `seply-theme` key the `ThemeProvider` writes.
- **Public directory** is `packages/ui/brand`, so the favicon and PWA icons are served from the site root and the brand stays one swappable folder. The web manifest (name, icons, theme colours) is generated from `vite.config.ts`; its two colours are the light `--background` and `--primary` tokens as hex, since a manifest can't read CSS.
- **Styles:** `main.tsx` imports `@seply/ui/globals.css`, then `@xyflow/react/dist/style.css`, then `@seply/views/canvas.css` (that order).
