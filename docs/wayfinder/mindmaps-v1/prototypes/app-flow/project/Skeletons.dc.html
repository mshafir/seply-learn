While a View builds

Each View shows its own shape first, then fills in

Learning path, Lineage, Cause & EffectFound Concepts stream in as nodes; placeholders hold their slots, so nothing jumps when the layout lands.
Comparison TableRows appear first; cells fill left to right as each Attribute is found. Unknowns stay “?” rather than guessed.
Anatomy, OutlineThe structure comes first (parts or headings), then techniques pin onto each part as they're placed.
MapThe map frames the area right away; pins drop in as places are geocoded, with open rings for ones still looking.
Timeline, RatesLanes and the axis are drawn first; spans and estimates slot in as their dates or ranges are pinned down.
When a View can't be builtSay why in plain words (“only one estimate per trend”), then offer Retry, a different View, or Remove. The other Views are unaffected.
