# Hub

A radial view around one Concept: rings of what's directly related, then what's related to that.

## What was tried

- **Statins → My situation.** Radial on the reader's own Concept over `implies` / `next-step`: their numbers, what they mean, then actions.
- **Family trip → The trip.** Radial on the chosen resort over `part-of`, `enjoys`, `similar-to` and `next-step`: each kid (person Concepts) `enjoys` activities, plus day trips and to-dos.
- **3D printer → For the kids.** Radial on the "Kids' value" goal: projects and safety.

## Why it fell flat

- The rings are ordered by graph distance, which isn't a meaningful axis to the reader. Numbers, meanings and actions all ended up in the same ring.
- Big rings (15–25 Concepts) spread labels far apart, so the view needed zooming to read, which defeats "at a glance".
- The same content read better as a list: "My situation" is already a topic in the statins Outline.

## Ideas for the next pass

- Group the ring into sectors by Relationship Type or Kind (numbers / meaning / actions), so position means something.
- For people Concepts (the kids), a small-multiples layout: one card per person listing what they'll enjoy.
- For "my situation", a checklist of next steps with dates might be the real View Type (actions with due dates, done/not done).
