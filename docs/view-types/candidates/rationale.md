# Rationale

A decision at the center with the reasons for it on one side, the trade-offs against it on the other, and the near-alternatives that almost won.

## What was tried

- **Family trip → Why this resort.** Radial around the decision over `reason`, `trade-off`, `chosen` and `similar-to`: "The family was excited about Region D", "An hour closer than the runner-up", "Verified price, a third of the budget", against the ~70°F pool and "Nothing programmed for the youngest", with the runner-up resort, another resort and others as alternatives.

## Why it fell flat

- A ring of short claims around one node doesn't say more than a bulleted pros/cons list would.
- Reasons, trade-offs and alternatives were distinguished only by edge colour, so the three groups didn't separate spatially.
- The strongest content (the the runner-up vs the chosen resort head-to-head) is a two-column comparison, which the radial layout hid.

## Ideas for the next pass

- Shape it like the Evidence View Type, which worked: the decision as the hub, reasons fanned out on one side and trade-offs on the other, alternatives below.
- Or fold it into Evidence entirely, with the decision as a claim ("the chosen resort is the right choice") and reasons and trade-offs as supports and challenges.
- A head-to-head mode: the chosen option against its runner-up, criterion by criterion (a two-row Comparison Table).
