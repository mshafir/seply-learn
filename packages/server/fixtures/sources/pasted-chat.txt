Can you explain what a prime number is?
ChatGPT said:
A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 whose only divisors are 1 and itself.

Examples: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11.
You said:
Is 1 prime?
ChatGPT said:
No. By convention 1 is not prime, which keeps factorisation into primes unique.
You said:
Why does uniqueness matter?
ChatGPT said:
Because the fundamental theorem of arithmetic says every number above 1 has exactly one prime factorisation, and many proofs lean on that.
