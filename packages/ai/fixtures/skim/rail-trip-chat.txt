## User

I'm planning a week in Portugal by train in early May: fly into Lisbon, fly out of Porto. I like old towns, food and a bit of walking. What would a good route look like?

## Assistant

A classic Lisbon-to-Porto week works well by train, since the main line runs up the coast. A balanced plan:

- **Days 1 to 3: Lisbon.** Alfama and the castle, Belém (the tower and the Jerónimos Monastery), and a day trip to **Sintra** (40 minutes by suburban train from Rossio) for the palaces.
- **Day 4: Óbidos or Tomar.** Óbidos is a walled medieval town; it's easier by bus from Lisbon (about 1 hour). Tomar has the Convent of Christ, a Templar castle, and a direct train (about 2 hours).
- **Days 5 to 6: Coimbra.** The old university, the library, and the steep old town. About 1 h 45 on the Alfa Pendular from Lisbon or 1 h from Tomar with a change at Entroncamento.
- **Days 7 to 8: Porto.** The Ribeira riverside, the Dom Luís I bridge, port cellars across the river in Vila Nova de Gaia, and the Douro valley if you have an extra day. Coimbra to Porto is 1 hour by fast train.

## User

Is the Douro valley worth it? How would I get there by train?

## Assistant

It's one of the most beautiful train rides in Europe. The Douro line runs from Porto's São Bento station to **Pinhão** (about 2 h 30) along the river through terraced vineyards. Sit on the right-hand side going east for river views.

In early May the vines are green and it's not yet hot. You can visit a quinta (wine estate) near Pinhão for a tasting, then take the train back. That makes a long day, so it works best as an extra day 9 or instead of one Porto day.

## User

What about tickets? Do I need to book ahead?

## Assistant

- **Alfa Pendular and Intercidades** (Lisbon, Coimbra, Porto): book on the CP website or app. Seats are reserved and cheaper when booked a few weeks ahead (a "promo" fare can be 30 to 50 percent off). Lisbon to Coimbra is about 25 to 35 euros full price.
- **Regional and urban trains** (Sintra, the Douro line, Tomar): no reservation, buy on the day at the station or with a contactless card on the Lisbon suburban lines. Sintra is about 2.40 euros each way; Porto to Pinhão about 11 euros.

The Douro line can be full on weekends in spring: arrive 20 minutes early.

## User

Where should I stay in Lisbon? I don't want to be climbing hills with a suitcase.

## Assistant

Lisbon is hilly everywhere, but some areas are easier:

- **Baixa** (downtown): flat, central, next to Rossio station for Sintra. The best choice with luggage.
- **Chiado**: stylish and central but uphill from Baixa.
- **Alfama**: charming, but steep cobbled lanes; lovely to walk in, hard with a suitcase.

In Porto, **Ribeira** is beautiful but steep; staying near **São Bento station** or on the flat area around Aliados is easier and puts you next to the Douro trains.

## User

How much time do I really need in Sintra, and which palace should I pick if I only do one?

## Assistant

Give Sintra a full day and start early (be on the 8:40 train or so); the crowds arrive by 10:30.

If you only do one:
- **Pena Palace** is the famous colourful one on the hilltop, with the best views; the most crowded, and you need a timed ticket.
- **Quinta da Regaleira** has the gardens with the initiation well, grottos and tunnels; more fun to explore on foot, and better if you like walking.

Many people do Regaleira in the morning, then walk or take the bus up to Pena for the afternoon slot.

## User

What should I eat in each place?

## Assistant

- **Lisbon**: pastéis de nata (the Belém bakery is the famous one), grilled sardines (more in June, but you'll find them), bacalhau (salt cod) dishes, and a ginjinha (cherry liqueur) at the tiny bars near Rossio.
- **Coimbra**: chanfana (goat stewed in red wine) and leitão (suckling pig) from the Bairrada area just north.
- **Porto**: the francesinha (a heavy sandwich with a beer and tomato sauce), tripas à moda do Porto, and port tastings in Gaia.
- **Douro**: the local wines, and a lunch at a quinta if you book ahead.

## User

Can you lay out the final plan with dates? Arrive Saturday 2 May, fly out Sunday 10 May.

## Assistant

- **Sat 2 May**: land in Lisbon, stay in Baixa, evening walk in Alfama.
- **Sun 3 May**: Belém in the morning, Chiado and the Bairro Alto in the evening.
- **Mon 4 May**: Sintra day trip (early train from Rossio).
- **Tue 5 May**: train to Tomar (Convent of Christ), overnight in Tomar.
- **Wed 6 May**: train to Coimbra (change at Entroncamento), afternoon in the old town.
- **Thu 7 May**: Coimbra University and the Joanina Library (book a timed slot); evening train to Porto.
- **Fri 8 May**: Porto: Ribeira, the bridge, Gaia port cellars.
- **Sat 9 May**: Douro line to Pinhão, quinta tasting, back in the evening (book the quinta ahead; weekend trains are busy).
- **Sun 10 May**: morning at the Livraria Lello or the market, then fly out of Porto.
